    Your recovery phrase

    Step 2 of 5 · Open

    Twelve to twenty-four words are the only backup. There is no company, no support desk and no reset. Whoever has the words has the ada.

    Practise the check your wallet will ask for, with a demo phrase. Then write your real one on paper and keep it offline.

    Be aware of the most common scams.

    Demo only
    cardano.org will never ask for a real phrase, and neither will anyone honest. These twelve words are not a real phrase.
    1. 1byron
    2. 2shelley
    3. 3goguen
    4. 4basho
    5. 5voltaire
    6. 6ouroboros
    7. 7lovelace
    8. 8plutus
    9. 9hydra
    10. 10mithril
    11. 11conway
    12. 12epoch

    Stake and vote

    Step 5 of 5 · Open

    Delegation moves rights, not ada. Your ada stays spendable, the pool produces blocks with it, your DRep votes with it. A vote delegation is also required before staking rewards can be withdrawn.

    Start with the stake pool: that delegation also registers your stake key. Then delegate your vote to a DRep. Both happen in your wallet.
