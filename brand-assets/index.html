    Usage Guidelines​

    Minimum Sizes

    Web64 px
    Tablet60 px
    Phone56 px
    Print20 mm

    Safe Area

    Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the width of six dots from the starburst icon. This ensures the logo remains legible and visually distinct in all applications.

    Do's & Don'ts

    Use only the specified brand colors
    Maintain the safe area around the logo
    The icon can be used as a standalone element
    Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
    Do not recolor the logo outside brand colors
    Do not use the wordmark without the icon

    Co-branding

    When the Cardano logo appears alongside a partner logo, the Cardano logo should be displayed at an equal or larger size to maintain brand prominence.
