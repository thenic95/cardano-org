    Transaction Tracking​

    Coverage Status

    During this reporting period, the Cardano network processed 2,445,701 total transactions. The 69 tracked entries account for 417,626 transactions (17.1% of network activity).

    Currently 21 of 103 apps listed on cardano.org have transaction tracking enabled via statsLabel.

    This leaderboard only tracks transactions that are identifiable on-chain through known smart contract addresses or metadata patterns. Many network transactions (simple transfers, staking operations, untracked apps) are not attributed to specific applications.

    Get Your App Tracked

    If your app's transactions aren't being tracked, you may need to make them identifiable on-chain. Learn how to set up tracking for your application.

    Once enabled, your app will appear on this leaderboard and gain visibility across the Cardano ecosystem. Tracking is open to all projects building on Cardano.
