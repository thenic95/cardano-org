    How to apply​

    Three steps, whichever program you choose

    1. Pick a program that fits

      Ideas go to Catalyst or a hackathon, products to an accelerator, and infrastructure work to Intersect or the treasury.

    2. Plan your milestones and budget

      Every program here pays against results, so be clear about what you'll ship, when, and what it costs.

    3. Apply on the official site

      Only use the links in the program details. No real program will ever ask for your recovery phrase.
