    Achievements

    A handpicked timeline of Ambassador wins since 2018.

    1. 2026

      cardano.org community translation

      Ambassadors localised cardano.org into multiple languages, making the project accessible to non-English audiences.

      Led by 5 Ambassadors

      cardano.org
    2. 2026

      Buidler Fest, Buenos Aires

      A flagship developer gathering in LATAM, organised and powered by the local Ambassador community.

      Led by 9 Ambassadors

      x.com
    3. 2025

      First Ambassador Retreat in Berlin

      The first dedicated Cardano Ambassador workshop gathers around 12 Ambassadors at Projektraum Drahnsdorf the weekend before the Berlin Summit. The retreat is funded by Project Catalyst Fund 13 and focuses on governance, collaboration and program direction.

      Led by Felix Weber, 12 Ambassadors

      lidonation.comx.com
    4. 2024

      First Cardano node at a LATAM university

      ALBA (Ada Labs for Blockchain Applications) brings the first university-run Cardano node online in Latin America.

      Led by Jose Iadiccico, Martin Ungar

      x.com
    5. 2024

      UTN Buenos Aires partnership

      Cardano enters Argentina's largest engineering university as an official education partner.

      Led by Jose Iadiccico

      cardanofoundation.org
    6. 2024

      Petrobras education partnership

      A landmark education partnership with one of the world's largest energy companies, opening blockchain training to Petrobras engineers.

      Led by Thiago Nunes

      cardanofoundation.org
    7. 2024

      Summit 2024 community events in 25+ countries

      Community-hosted events accompanied the Dubai Summit across 25+ countries, from Addis Ababa to Amsterdam, Buenos Aires, Sydney, Seoul, Tokyo, New York and London.

      Led by Cardano community and Ambassadors

      adapulse.io
    8. 2023

      Summit 2023 satellite events in 25 cities

      Around the Dubai main stage, the community organised 25 satellite events worldwide, with hosts in cities like Taipei, Seoul, Kumasi, Buenos Aires and Ho Chi Minh City.

      Led by Cardano community and Ambassadors

      adapulse.io
    9. 2022

      30+ Cardano Summit community events

      A worldwide network of community-led Summit events extending the official program across continents.

      Led by 15+ Ambassadors

      forum.cardano.org
    10. 2022

      Ledger Live Integration

      Secure Ada management lands in Ledger's flagship app, driven by an Ambassador-led Catalyst proposal.

      Led by Ambassador Guild

      ledger.comprojectcatalyst.io
    11. 2021

      LVL2 Catalyst Reviewers

      Sustained, top-tier proposal review across multiple Catalyst funds, shaping how community funding gets allocated.

      Led by 20+ Ambassadors

      docs.projectcatalyst.io
    12. 2021

      Summit 2021 expands to 37 cities

      The hybrid Cardano Summit was supported by 38 virtual and live community-led events across 37 cities, hosted by 57+ volunteer organisers including Cardano Ambassadors.

      Led by Cardano Ambassadors and 57+ community volunteers

      forum.cardano.org
    13. 2020

      SPO Scripts for stake-pool operators

      Martin Lang publishes the SPO Scripts, a widely used shell-script toolkit for Cardano stake-pool operators. Covers pool creation, token transfers, Catalyst voter registration and, through the Voltaire era, DRep management and on-chain governance with hardware-wallet support.

      Led by Martin Lang

      github.comgithub.com
    14. 2020

      Phase 2 rewards program launches

      Phase 2 of the Ambassador Program rolls out ada-denominated rewards. At rollout the program counts 58 Ambassadors across 26 countries and 28 languages.

      Led by Cardano Foundation, 58 Ambassadors at rollout

      forum.cardano.orgcardanofoundation.org
    15. 2019

      Cardano on the Rocks

      Markus Gufler demonstrates running a Cardano stake pool on an 18-watt Rock Pi single-board computer. The educational project ships hardware kits, a 3D-printed enclosure and a step-by-step guide for operators.

      Led by Markus Gufler

      forum.cardano.orggithub.com
    16. 2018

      Ambassador Program launches

      The Cardano Foundation formally announces the Cardano Ambassador Program, defining the first four roles: Meetup Organizers, Translators, Content Creators and Moderators.

      Led by Cardano Foundation, founding Ambassador cohort

      forum.cardano.org
