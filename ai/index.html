    x402 on Cardano

    x402 on Cardano is more powerful than x402 anywhere else.

    +

    Native support

    x402 on Cardano natively supports the Masumi Smart Contract.

    The Cardano Foundation is a member of the x402 Foundation, the industry body behind the x402 standard, alongside Coinbase, Cloudflare, Google, Visa, and Mastercard.

    The Masumi Smart Contract is the escrow at the core of the payment standard for AI agents on Cardano: funds are locked in the contract, released when the work is delivered, and refunded when it isn't. Every decision is logged on-chain.

    It is an open standard, not a product: a plain escrow contract with no fees and no owner, specified as part of the official x402 scheme for Cardano. Plugged into x402, every HTTP payment gets that protection built in.

    The exact scheme on Cardano supports three asset transfer methods:

    • Address-to-address payments similar to the regular x402 specification on other chains.
    • The Masumi Smart Protocol which adds decentralised refund mechanics and decision logging.
    • Script payments paying to scripts with parameters applied while building the transaction.
